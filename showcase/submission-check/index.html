    The Allyspan Journal

    Lived experience held to the evidence

    The Allyspan Journal publishes research and policy alongside lived experiences and perspectives from people of all backgrounds.

    Read the Journal
    Delivery check - use a test manuscript only

    You can try the form and review your details. Nothing is sent or saved. Sending will open after private submission handling and receipt emails are ready.

    What happens after you submit

    1. An editor reads your complete submission. We aim to give an initial response within 15 business days.
    2. If it is a fit, we work with you on revisions.
    3. Research articles receive independent methodological review by two independent reviewers. Reviewers are confidential to authors; publication waits if suitable reviewers are unavailable.
    4. We publish with the article type and actual review status stated, after your final proof approval.
