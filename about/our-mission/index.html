    About Us

    Our Mission

    Connecting people with support.
    Working toward a more equitable community.

    Why we exist

    Our purpose

    Allyspan’s mission is to reduce barriers to community resources, challenge oppression and social exclusion, and support people in pursuing goals they define for themselves.

    We are building an organization that brings together practical guidance, connections to appropriate agencies, community education, and thoughtful follow-through. Every person deserves to be heard, treated with respect, and included in decisions that affect their life.

    What guides us

    Anti-oppression and our values

    Anti-oppression means recognizing how racism, poverty, ableism, discrimination, and unequal power can shape someone’s access to support. We aim to challenge these barriers in the way we listen, design programs, and work with others.

    Choice and dignity. We respect people’s identities, experiences, and right to make their own decisions.

    Access and inclusion. We seek to make information understandable and participation more accessible across languages, backgrounds, and abilities.

    Accountability. We welcome feedback, acknowledge limits, and use what we learn to improve our work.

    Our beginnings

    The experiences behind Allyspan

    Allyspan’s direction is informed by its founder’s experiences and a commitment to building more respectful, accessible systems of support.

    These experiences inform a central concern: how institutions respond to people, and what happens when someone faces barriers to help. Allyspan carries that concern into an organizational commitment to practical support, human dignity, and community connection.

    As we grow, the perspectives of people using our programs, community partners, staff, and volunteers will help shape our work.

    Our direction

    Building our mission into practice

    We are developing our programs step by step, with care for the people they are intended to serve. Our mission is a commitment that guides this work as Allyspan takes shape.
