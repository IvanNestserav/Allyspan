    Introduction to Classroom Studio

    One lesson. More ways to participate.
    A basic guide for multilingual classrooms.

    Meet the Studio

    Create once.
    Connect across languages.

    Classroom Studio is Allyspan’s online presentation workspace for educators. Create visual lessons, add multilingual text, and share a lesson through a QR code or link so students can follow along in an available language they understand.

    Teachers sign in with Google to create and save presentations. Students can open shared lessons without an account, choose a lesson language, and move through the slides at their own pace.

    For teachers

    Create your
    first lesson.

    1. Sign in. Open Classroom Studio and sign in with Google. Find saved lessons under My presentations.
    2. Choose a starting point. Browse templates or create a blank presentation. Give your lesson a name you will recognize later.
    3. Build your slides. Edit the English text, add images, and move objects into place. Keep each slide focused and leave room for translations.
    4. Choose languages. Open Languages in the editor and select those your class needs. Translate your text and review the wording for accuracy and classroom meaning.
    5. Save and preview. Confirm the save status says Saved before closing. Select Present to check the lesson before class.

    Share and present

    Invite your
    students in.

    Keep Include QR slide 0 enabled for a welcome screen. Selecting Present turns sharing on and displays the lesson QR code and link. You can also open Share & QR in the editor.

    Use QR options to copy the complete lesson link, choose a QR color, or download the QR image. Test the QR code on a second device before class.

    The same link continues to work as you update a lesson. Anyone with the link can view it while sharing is on. Use Turn sharing off in the sharing window when you want to stop access.

    For learners

    Scan. Choose.
    Follow along.

    Scan the teacher’s QR code or open the complete shared lesson link. No student sign-in is needed.

    Choose an available Lesson language, then use Next and Back to explore the slides. When available, show English alongside the translation. An internet connection is needed to open a shared lesson.

    Before class

    A few useful habits.

    Check translations. Review important wording before teaching, particularly instructions and unfamiliar terms.

    Keep sharing intentional. Share only material appropriate for everyone who may receive the link. Presenter notes stay in the editor and are not included in the shared student lesson.

    Prepare an offline copy. Download the presentation with its saved translations and included media. Extract the ZIP, then open index.html in a modern browser. A Home Screen shortcut still needs internet.

    Test your display. Use Full screen for the teacher screen. Phone browser controls and fullscreen behavior vary by device.

    Help and feedback

    A platform
    we are improving together.

    Classroom Studio continues to develop, so features and controls may change. To report an issue, sign in, return to the home workspace, and select Report a problem in the sidebar.

    Explain what you were trying to do, what happened, and which device and browser you used. Include any error message, but leave out student names and private information.
